# Top Coffee Roasters — Aggregated Ranking (2024–2026)

Compiled 2026-07-07. This document aggregates coffee roasters that appeared across
multiple independently published "best/top coffee roasters" lists and industry awards
from the last two years (mid-2024 through mid-2026), then ranks them by how many
distinct sources named them.

## Methodology & limitations

- Sources were identified via web search across specialty-coffee publications,
  award bodies, and consumer "best of" roundups (Roast Magazine, Sprudge, Coffee
  Review, Forbes Vetted, Chowhound, Coffee Crafters, Corner Coffee Store, The Daily
  Meal, Colipse, Coffee Bros., Friends of Marlin, and the Global Coffee Awards).
- Direct page scraping was not available in this environment (outbound fetches to
  these sites were blocked by network policy), so roaster names were extracted from
  search-engine result summaries rather than full article text. Large panel-voted
  lists (e.g. Roastful's "Top 100" and Coffee Bros.' "51 Best") could only be
  partially recovered — their counts here likely undercount their true overlap with
  other lists.
- "Appearances" counts a roaster once per distinct source/list, regardless of its
  rank within that list. It is not a rigorous statistical sample — treat it as a
  directional signal of which names recur most across the specialty coffee press,
  not a definitive industry ranking.
- 12 source lists/awards were used (see Sources table below).

## Notes for future refinement

If direct web fetching becomes available in a future session (or this is run with
a tool that can retrieve full page contents from these sites), the ranking should
be redone by pulling complete roaster lists — especially from the large panel
lists (Roastful's Top 100, Coffee Bros.' 51) — since those were only partially
captured here and would meaningfully shift the aggregate ranking.
